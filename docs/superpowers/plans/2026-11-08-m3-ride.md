# M3: The Ride (Màn 3–5) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** After leaving the room, ride the black AB 2026 through a small Vietnamese street map: pick up Em (Màn 3), carry her to her office by 8:25 without upsetting her (Màn 4), sprint to Anh's office by 8:30 (Màn 5), then show the spec's endings.

**Architecture:** Pure modules in `src/ride/` (bike kinematics, city map data, traffic, ride rules, endings) with Vitest tests; one `useRide` zustand store for the ride (bike state, stage, events) next to `useStory` (clock, mood, hygiene, tasks). The street scene is a separate lazily-loaded chunk (`src/ride/StreetScene.tsx`) mounted when `phase === 'street'`, so the room loads first. City geometry reuses the merged-parts pipeline (`Batched`/`PartsMesh`) to keep draw calls low.

**Tech Stack:** as M1/M2. **Ruling:** no Rapier — a custom arcade kinematic bike (circle vs boxes, reusing `resolveCircleBoxes`) keeps the bundle ~2 MB smaller, deterministic and testable; spec §7 named Rapier for "playful, not simulated" driving, which this satisfies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-oneday-design.md` §4.1, §4.4–4.7, §5, §6, §8.

## Global Constraints
- Copy in `src/strings.ts`; Em speaks as "em", calls Anh "anh".
- Deadlines: Em's office by 8:25, Anh's office by 8:30 (game minutes 505 / 510).
- The street clock runs faster than the room clock: `STREET_SPEED = 18` game s per real s (tunable). Target: leaving the room at ~7:55 and riding decently reaches Em's office ~8:18 and Anh's office ~8:24.
- Phones: landscape, touch buttons ≥ 56 px for driving; desktop W/S/A/D (+ arrows).
- Draw calls in the street ≤ 60 on phones (merged city, ≤ 12 traffic vehicles).
- Bike: black matte AB-like scooter, **no Honda logo or "Air Blade" text**.

## Review Focus
1. Pausing (help) must freeze the bike, traffic and the street clock → test `street clock and traffic stop while paused`.
2. Crashing into a wall at speed must not tunnel → test `bike never ends inside a collider at max speed`.
3. Stopping at Em's house while she is already aboard, or arriving at offices in the wrong order, must not double-trigger → test `stage transitions happen once and in order`.
4. Restart from any street stage resets bike, traffic, stage and Em → test `restart from the street returns to the alarm`.
5. Touch: holding Gas with one thumb and steering with the other must both work (multi-touch) → test on the controls hook.

## Tasks (each: failing tests → implement → green suite → build → commit → push)

1. **Stages & street clock.** `phase: 'street'`, `stage: 'toEm' | 'withEm' | 'sprint' | 'done'`; `leave` now enters the street; `tick` uses `STREET_SPEED` in the street. Tests: leaving enters the street at stage `toEm`; street tick speed; paused freezes; restart resets.
2. **Bike kinematics.** Accel 4 m/s², max 13 m/s (≈47 km/h), brake 9 m/s², coast drag, steering rate scaled by speed, reverse ≤ 2 m/s; collision with circle r 0.45 vs boxes; events `crash` (impact > 4 m/s), `hardBrake` (decel > 7 m/s² while > 5 m/s). Tests for each, plus no tunnelling at max speed with dt 0.05.
3. **City map.** 3×3 block grid (~360 × 300 m) of roads 8 m wide with 3 m sidewalks; tube-house buildings fill blocks; markers `home`, `emHouse`, `emOffice`, `anhOffice` (emOffice→anhOffice ≈ 110 m); an alley shortcut 3 m wide; potholes; 4 traffic lights. Tests: markers on roads; flood-fill route exists between consecutive markers for r 0.45; alley is shorter than the road route.
4. **City rendering.** Merged parts: asphalt with lane dashes, sidewalks, colourful tube houses with balconies and signboards, power poles with wire bundles, trees, xôi stall, bus stop, markers (glowing rings + labels). Draw-call test on parts groups (≤ 3 materials) and a perf check in emulation.
5. **Bike + riders.** AB-like scooter (sharp front fairing, LED headlight, mirrors, step-through, black matte, grey wheels), Anh seated with hands on bars, helmet if taken, raincoat if worn; Em character (long wavy dark-brown hair, short-sleeve blouse, long trousers) standing at her door and riding pillion. Chase camera with speed-based FOV.
6. **Traffic, lights, potholes, rain.** Kinematic motorbikes/cars/bus on lane loops; their boxes collide with the bike; red light crossing event; pothole bump event above 6 m/s; rain particles + puddles when `weather === 'rain'`.
7. **Controls & HUD.** Keyboard W/S/A/D; touch pedals (◀ ▶ left, Phanh/Ga right, multi-touch); HUD speed, clock, next-stop arrow + distance, Em's mood heart when aboard; help text per stage.
8. **Rules & dialogue.** Pickup when stopped (< 1 m/s) within 4 m of Em: helmet check (no helmet for Em: mood −15), outfit (disaster −5, hint match +5 line), wet without raincoat (hygiene −15, Em line). With Em: crash −10, hard brake −3 ("Chạy từ từ thôi!"), pothole −5, red light −5; drop-off before 8:25 +5 else −20. Speech bubbles above Em. Arrival at Anh's office → stage `done` → ending.
9. **Endings.** `pickEnding` priority: overslept → late (> 8:30) → Em upset (mood < 40 or dropped after 8:25) → forgot to brush → perfect (all tasks, on time, mood ≥ 70) → ok. Ending card shows title, time, stats, task list, Em's last text ("Mai em tự đi làm ko cần đón" for upset), restart.
10. **Ship.** Full suite, build, emulation checks (drive, pickup, drop-off, ending), push, temporary Vercel deploy, fresh review.
